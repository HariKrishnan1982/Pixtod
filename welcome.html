FETCH_IMG_ — INSTALLED

A viewfinder for the pictures already on your screen.

Hold the cursor over any picture on any site. Three marks appear in the corner: view it full size, save it, or copy it. Six frames on the right, each one a different part of the toolkit.

Source, issues & suggestions
